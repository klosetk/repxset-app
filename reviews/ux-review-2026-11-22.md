# Rep Counter UI/UX review (Nov 22, 2026)

Reviewed every flow at 390px (light) and 360px (dark) in a headless browser, plus a source read. No JS errors. Not verified: actual audio playback, wake lock on a real phone, a real screen reader.

## High
1. Profile opened from the plan: changing rest/pace/unit there doesn't update the plan being edited; unit lives in three places that disagree.
2. Custom exercises vanish if untapped; changing category silently clears all picks.
3. Goal auto-tag doesn't happen on the default "All my goals" tab, despite onboarding promising it.
4. Every toggle redraws the page and drops keyboard/screen-reader focus.
5. On/Off and lb/kg buttons have no accessible names.
6. Subtitle bar floats over content (tip card, Done buttons) and lingers after leaving the workout.

## Medium
- No visible paused state; elapsed clock is the hero instead of the rep count; captions show rep numbers out of sync.
- Form-check screen has a blank clock gap and repeated text.
- Finish set at 0 reps counts as a set; Skip rest jumps straight to work with no countdown.
- Personal bests are saved when a workout starts, not when lifted.
- Switching lb/kg doesn't convert weights.
- Mislabels: "Back to workout" from plan screens, Saved "Start" opens the plan, "start screen" wording.
- Voice wording differs (Muted / voice muted / Voice off); header aria-labels don't match visible text.
- "Hear this coach" is silent when the voice is muted; unexplained "Guess" tags; odd goal keyword matches.
- Home tabs float mid-screen.

## Low
- Tap targets under 44px (header chips, steppers, chips, slider); hidden-scroll goal tabs; small tags; low-contrast blue goal dot in dark mode.
- "1 sets" grammar, "0m" for short workouts, charts and steppers missing labels, Add/Save chips announced as "pressed".
- Delete confirm has no cancel; 0-set workouts show "Done."; Enter doesn't advance onboarding; unit sits under "Sound"; exercise order can't be changed; empty Analytics stacks empty cards.
